Central

Use one substantial cultural anchor, then notice the city operating between the office towers and hillside streets.

How this place became itself

Central was part of the north-shore settlement whose first government land auction took place in 1841; the wider City of Victoria was named in 1843. Tai Kwun makes the colonial machinery of policing, magistracy and imprisonment physically visible.

The financial centre is also a steep pedestrian landscape of offices, small streets, conservation projects and international art. Its present identity cannot be reduced to either British nostalgia or luxury consumption.

With a camera: Public steps, street crossings, layered signage and the contrast between polished business streets and Tai Kwun’s enclosure. Avoid blocking stair landings.
