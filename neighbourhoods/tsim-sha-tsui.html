Tsim Sha Tsui

Do not avoid it simply because it is famous: the ferry and public art museum earn their place.

How this place became itself

The harbour edge and its transport and civic buildings helped make this a gateway between Kowloon, the island and the wider world. Conservation debates around 1881 Heritage show how commercial reuse can preserve monuments while altering their context.

Tourist hotels and luxury retail coexist with major public museums and a working ferry terminal. The famous panorama is worthwhile, but it is more revealing when connected to transport, art and the people using the waterfront.

With a camera: Ferry arrival, public promenade and the museum’s surroundings. Let harbour traffic and human scale animate the skyline.
