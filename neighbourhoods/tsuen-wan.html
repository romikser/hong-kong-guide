Tsuen Wan

Choose it for a focused art/material-history excursion, not just another converted shopping complex.

How this place became itself

A town with Hakka farming/fishing histories became a textile-industrial centre in the 1950s and Hong Kong’s first new town. The Nan Fung mills opened in this industrial expansion; CHAT now interprets its material and labour history.

Dense housing, commercial infrastructure and former industrial sites make this a different Hong Kong from the island’s heritage streets. Its contemporary-art value is especially strong when linked to textiles, labour and reuse.

On foot / with a camera: Public approach to The Mills and the retained factory architecture; use exhibitions to connect attractive industrial textures with the people who worked there.

CHAT at The Mills

special-interest

A strong special-interest excursion where the old Nan Fung textile mills are the subject, not merely a fashionable shell. Contemporary textile art and accounts of factory labour connect material culture to the manufacturing that built postwar Hong Kong.

2/F, The Mills, 45 Pak Tin Par Street, Tsuen Wan
