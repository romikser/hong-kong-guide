Neighbourhood / history & present identity

Wan Chai

An unusually complete area for linking living history, street photography and contemporary art.

How this place became itself

Wan Chai was part of the early City of Victoria, but its history is not merely colonial. The Blue House site carried Chinese medical and community uses, and its occupied conservation project centres the survival of social relationships alongside buildings.

Commercial streets, wet-market life, bars, contemporary galleries and hillside housing share a tight footprint. Blue House’s community-led account gives a different perspective from the district’s nightlife stereotype.

On foot / with a camera: Stone Nullah Lane and public streets around Blue House, then the stepped route toward Sik On Street if visiting the gallery. Respect residential no-photo areas.
