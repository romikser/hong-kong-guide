Kennedy Town

Choose it for waterfront observation and a slower walk, not a compulsory coffee-shop photograph.

How this place became itself

Named for Governor Arthur Kennedy, the western-end district’s modern growth is tied to reclamation and later transport-led redevelopment. Local accounts connect the recent property and restaurant boom to the arrival of the MTR.

It is now both a residential area and a popular waterfront destination. The sea edge provides breathing space, while the inland streets show the mixture of older working-city fabric and newer development.

With a camera: Public waterfront and inland residential streets; use public access and do not trespass into working or closed waterfront facilities.
