Quarry Bay

Use Para Site as the reason to come; the district’s density then becomes context rather than a checklist image.

How this place became itself

Photography gap: no suitable reviewed area photograph in this edition. No substitute skyline is used.

Quarry Bay was a major industrial company town: Swire’s Taikoo sugar refinery opened in 1884, with worker housing and related infrastructure around it. This industrial past complicates today’s image of an office and residential district.

Corporate redevelopment, older dense housing and independent art sit in close proximity. Para Site gives a reason to look beyond the viral composite-building photograph.

On foot / with a camera: Public King’s Road streets and visible layers of different-scale buildings. Do not assume residential courtyards are public viewing platforms.
