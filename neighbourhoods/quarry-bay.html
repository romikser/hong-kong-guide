Quarry Bay

A company-town and dense-residential landscape detour; no longer justify a visit by a promised Para Site gallery.

How this place became itself

Quarry Bay was a major industrial company town: Swire’s Taikoo sugar refinery opened in 1884, with worker housing and related infrastructure around it. This industrial past complicates today’s image of an office and residential district.

Corporate redevelopment and older dense housing coexist. Para Site formerly occupied an industrial building here, but its official notice now announces citywide programming from October 2026 through 2027.

With a camera: Public King’s Road streets and visible layers of different-scale buildings. Do not assume residential courtyards are public viewing platforms.
