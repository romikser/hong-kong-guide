Causeway Bay

Worth observing as contemporary popular urban culture; no need to spend a day inside interchangeable malls.

How this place became itself

The wider area’s development connects reclaimed shoreline, trade, industry and mass consumption. Local design history uses the arrival of Japanese department stores and Hong Kong’s silk industry to show how retail and manufacturing were once interwoven.

Shopping remains the loudest identity, but trams and the dense pedestrian environment reveal a city in motion. The more intimate Tai Hang streets nearby provide a useful contrast without pretending they are the same neighbourhood.

With a camera: Public crossings, tram corridors and layers of advertisements; work the changing scale between broad shopping roads and smaller side streets.
