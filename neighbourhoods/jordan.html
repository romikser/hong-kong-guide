Jordan

An accessible place to pair street observation with a small exhibition or opera nearby.

How this place became itself

Jordan belongs to the wider Yau Ma Tei urban story in which old shoreline, shipping, streets and reclamation produced today’s inland commercial grid. Modern station/neighbourhood labels should not be mistaken for ancient fixed boundaries.

Nathan Road’s traffic and side-street businesses coexist with Eaton HK’s contemporary cultural programming. The area works as an everyday urban link, not only a base for looking at Temple Street souvenirs.

With a camera: Public streets between Nathan Road, Shanghai Street and the cultural venues; do not assume Temple Street guarantees a meaningful purchase.
