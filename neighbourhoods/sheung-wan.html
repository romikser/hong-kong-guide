Sheung Wan

Probably the strongest compact match for photographs, art, history and meaningful printed objects.

How this place became itself

Tai Ping Shan became a densely occupied Chinese settlement in early colonial Hong Kong. The 1894 plague and subsequent clearance reshaped its fabric; trading streets farther downhill connected migration, finance and commodity supply.

The district now combines worship, older wholesale trades, independent galleries, design businesses and rising-rent café culture. “Old meets new” is visible, but also describes a conflict about who can still afford to remain.

On foot / with a camera: Tai Ping Shan, Pound Lane and the step streets; move downhill toward the wholesale streets to keep the story broader than photogenic cafés.
