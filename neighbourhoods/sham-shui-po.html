Sham Shui Po

A core area for materials, meaningful objects and photographs of a city that still makes and fixes things.

How this place became itself

The district’s maker culture grew through textiles, electronics, repair and small orders, not through the recent invention of a design “hub.” Local oral histories connect shops, workshops and streets to postwar industrial production and migration.

Working trade and diverse communities now coexist with design businesses and café-led reinvention. The important tension is whether the practical supply networks and lower-income residents can survive rising costs.

With a camera: Public market streets around Apliu Street and fabric/notion suppliers; look for relationships between materials, labour and small workshops rather than pictures of poverty.
