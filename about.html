Hong Kong / first review edition

Evidence & limitations

A useful first review edition, not a fully operator-verified or fully photographed final release. The evidence is shown; gaps are not converted into certainty.

What is in this edition

184 distinct cards from 188 selected source records: four same-branch coffee/food overlaps were merged. Coffee/breakfast: 44 selected records (29 editorial recommendations, 15 with gaps). Food: 50 editorial picks with explicit verification gaps. Shops/music: 53 recommended records; 7 conditional records excluded. Culture: 17 art spaces and 24 experiences. Category memberships overlap; the overall total does not.

Excluded from recommendations and every export: 17 coffee verification holds, 1 coffee reserve, 7 conditional shopping/music records, 2 separate food reserves and the rejected/deferred candidate ledgers. None is counted as a verified recommendation.

Verification is field-specific

Operator pages, Michelin, institutional pages, specialist local reporting and a limited set of accessible social captions support different facts. An indexed operator listing is not a live page check; current web presence is not telephone confirmation. 97 cards carry an explicit verification-gap notice. Even the other cards are snapshots, not guarantees of opening. Missing prices and kitchen hours stay unknown.

Research is strongest in Hong Kong Island and urban Kowloon; New Territories, islands and South Asian/Thai late-night food remain thinner. Breakfast service windows are not uniformly operator-verified. The 20 core histories vary in depth; 25 additional area/precinct/excursion pages provide focused context, not full historical coverage. No ownership nationality is inferred from names or language; beneficial ownership has not been comprehensively cleared.

Social discovery limits

TikTok access was insufficient for a meaningful caption/video audit. No unseen TikTok recommendation or footage is invented. Instagram evidence is limited to accessible profiles/captions, and does not establish unseen reels, comments, independence or a complete creator calibration.

Food preferences

No goat/sheep dairy dish recommendations. This is not an allergen-safety guide: confirm ingredients and preparation with the venue.

Build-time link check

468 distinct external source/programme links were fetched: 397 returned HTTP 200 and 71 had blocking, throttling, network/TLS errors or other responses. Three source links returned 404 (Double Q contact page, a Wonderwall project page and the old Little Dot tourism listing). Source evidence is retained with access warnings in details; successful retrieval is not proof of every fact or current operation. All 58 distinct embedded photo URLs loaded in the browser from the guide origin during QA.

Maps & complete exports

Apple Maps is the primary link on every card. All 184 links are explicit name/address searches; 0 direct POI pins have been resolved. No shared Apple Guide is represented as complete. Verify the returned name, exact branch and entrance before navigation; ferry routes, landscapes, changing exhibitions and precincts are not exact shop pins.

Google links are shareable search URLs, not Saved-list membership. Every city, area and type scope has three complete CSV downloads derived from the same card membership. Apple CSV contains individual search links, not an importable Apple Guide. Google My Maps CSV uses Name and Location columns; on the My Maps website, create a map, import that CSV, choose Location for location and Name for labels, then manually review ambiguous pins. Importing requires your action and does not create a normal Google Saved list. No Google account writes, automations or recurring jobs were performed. Official import instructions.

Photography: what is actually shown

54/184 distinct cards have genuine reviewed operator-hosted images, including labelled product, historical-installation and service images; 130 are text-only. 19/45 area pages have reviewed images: four sourced district/landmark images from Hong Kong Tourism Board, the others explicitly labelled operator-landmark area context. This is not yet a photo-led guide.

Images are embedded remotely from operator/tourism sources rather than copied into this repository. The caption gives the subject, exact source and available credit; no open licence or permission is asserted. Crediting is not a grant of rights. Hosts may block or withdraw embeds; remaining photo sourcing and permissions are a release gap. Third-party food-blog and Zolima photographs with uncleared reuse were excluded. No Maps user photos, stock substitutes, invented images or generic area images presented as named venues.

A build-time contact-sheet review rejected logos, an illustration, a generic gallery contact image and an Eaton dining-room photograph that did not establish Terrible Baby. A venue’s historical display or product photo does not establish today’s programme or stock.
