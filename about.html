Photography: measured, not claimed complete.

64/214 unique cards carry reviewed images. 45 show a venue/interior/landscape; 19 are explicitly described as food/product/collection views. 150 cards remain text-only. 9/18 core-area tiles have genuine area photographs or labelled landmark context. No generic skyline is represented as a venue.

The final pass researched 77 additional official sites and visually reviewed 120 candidates. Logos, menus, abstract posters, unrelated landing images and ambiguous multi-branch shots were rejected. Credits identify the operator or tourism board, and name an individual only when established. Older programme/installation images are not evidence of the December show.

Images remain remotely hosted by source operators. Copyright is retained; no general reuse licence or publication permission is established. Source-level rights caveats appear beside each photograph. Remote loading does not grant reuse rights and can break if the source changes. They have not been copied into the repository. Source-CDN width requests are bounded where supported; lazy loading and fixed aspect sizes reduce layout shift. Text-only coverage is disclosed rather than filled with unrelated images.

Maps & useful exports.

Apple Maps search is the default card action. Every link is a name-and-address search, explicitly not a verified POI or shared Apple Guide. Zero exact POIs have been verified. Google search links and My Maps-import CSVs are optional; no account/list writes have been performed.

Exports exist for the whole city, every core neighbourhood, each focused location, the three aggregate geographic scales, and each category. Each venue scope has complete, Apple-link and Google My Maps variants with identical membership. Import Name and Location into a map and review every match; ambiguous addresses can land on the wrong branch. Dated programmes have their own separately labelled CSV and are not mixed into venue counts.

Editorial & practical limits.

Anchors justify time; nearby cards are alternatives; special-interest detours need a particular reason. The research checks operator access, food service, current branches and ownership where possible; unknown ownership is not silently certified. No goat/sheep-dairy dish is recommended. Local-source and creator discovery informs selection, but followers, Michelin listings and fame do not establish current opening hours.

Check holidays, tickets, installation periods, weather and ferry/trail access before travel. No festival recurrence, race meeting or performance time has been inferred. This is a browsable public library, not a private itinerary.
