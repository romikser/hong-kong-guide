Tsuen Wan

A town with Hakka farming/fishing histories became a textile-industrial centre in the 1950s and Hong Kong’s first new town. The Nan Fung mills opened in this industrial expansion; CHAT now interprets its material and labour history.

Dense housing, commercial infrastructure and former industrial sites make this a different Hong Kong from the island’s heritage streets. Its contemporary-art value is especially strong when linked to textiles, labour and reuse.
