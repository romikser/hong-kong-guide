Diamond Hill / Experiences

Nan Lian Garden and Chi Lin surroundings

nearby

A deliberate composed landscape against high-rise Kowloon: timber structures, rocks and carefully managed planting. Worthwhile for visual rhythm and a quieter scale, but do not present Tang-style design as a surviving Tang-dynasty garden.

Conditional recommendation. LCSD confirms modern Tang-style garden and 3.5-hectare area; current Chi Lin interior access not separately verified.

M+: visual culture, design and moving image

anchor

One of the best big-ticket anchors for these interests: a museum that treats twentieth- and twenty-first-century art, design, architecture and moving image as connected visual culture. Combine the collection with a film or the Mediatheque rather than only photographing the facade.
